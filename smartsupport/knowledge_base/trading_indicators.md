# Stateira Labs Trading Indicators

Stateira Labs develops high-quality, professional technical indicators designed to give traders an edge in the markets. These indicators are compatible with major charting platforms like TradingView and ThinkOrSwim.

## Available Indicators

### 1. ATR Level Indicator
This indicator visualizes dynamic support and resistance levels based on Average True Range (ATR) calculations. It helps traders identify potential reversal zones, breakout thresholds, and optimal stop-loss placement by factoring in the asset's current volatility rather than relying purely on static historical price points.

### 2. Pulse Pivot Matrix (PPM)
The Pulse Pivot Matrix features a unique 5-EMA (Exponential Moving Average) ribbon system designed for trend analysis. It smooths out price action to clearly identify the prevailing trend direction and momentum. When the EMA ribbons fan out, it signals strong momentum, whereas tightly packed ribbons indicate consolidation.

### 3. Xeij Volume
Xeij Volume provides dynamic volume insights that go beyond standard volume bars. It helps traders distinguish between buy and sell pressure, identify volume anomalies, and confirm whether price movements are supported by adequate participation from market participants.
