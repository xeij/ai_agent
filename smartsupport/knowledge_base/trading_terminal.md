# Stateira Labs Trading Terminal

The Stateira Labs Trading Terminal is a professional, web-based platform designed for equity traders and investors. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools all within a single interface, eliminating the need to jump between different websites for market research and analysis.

## Key Features
- **Live Market Data:** Real-time stock quotes and customizable watchlists.
- **Advanced Charting:** Interactive TradingView-style charts.
- **Market Sentiment:** Fear & Greed Index with historical data, and real-time stock market heatmaps.
- **Upcoming Events:** Earnings calendar with filters for Today, This Week, and This Month, as well as an upcoming IPO tracker.
- **Deep Research:** SEC filings browser, insider trading tracker, and options chain data.
- **Fundamental Analysis:** Financial statements (income, cash flow, earnings), relative valuation tools side-by-side security comparisons.
- **News Integration:** Real-time market news feed, research reports, and analyst ratings.
