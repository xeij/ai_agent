# Stateira Labs Development Services

In addition to our software products, Stateira Labs offers premium consulting and development services for businesses looking to build scalable, modern technology solutions.

## Full-Stack Web Development
We build responsive, high-performance web applications tailored to your business needs.
- **Frontend & Backend Technologies:** React, Next.js, and Node.js.
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** We design and deploy robust, scalable architectures on Amazon Web Services (AWS).
- **API Development:** Custom RESTful and GraphQL API design and implementation.
- **Design:** Expert UI/UX design to ensure applications are both beautiful and highly usable.

## Blockchain & Crypto Development
We help traditional businesses integrate with Web3 and assist crypto startups in building secure decentralized protocols.
- **Smart Contract Development:** Writing secure, efficient smart contracts (e.g., Solidity) for EVM-compatible networks.
- **Smart Contract Auditing:** Security reviews of existing contracts to identify vulnerabilities before deployment.
- **Crypto Payments Integration:** Helping e-commerce and SaaS platforms accept cryptocurrency payments seamlessly.
